# Decision brief (Supervisor)
Use when a Lead reports `DECISION_NEEDED`, when a dispute is about product scope, material cost,
external effects, or irreversible risk, or when an intent-record assumption turned out false.
1. One line: what cannot proceed and why now.
2. Options (2–3): what changes in the outcome, cost or risk, what it rules out later.
3. Your recommendation and its consequence in one sentence, with its evidence. What the room
   does if no answer arrives: it builds the recommendation so the other answer stays a small
   change, and goes on; it holds only what cannot be undone.
4. What the Lead does meanwhile (unrelated ready work, or nothing).
5. Deliver with `DECISION_NEEDED` as the first line of the message and the `WAITING ON YOU:`
   row of your room-state block. Never bury it in a status paragraph. Return to the Owner only
   what the Lead should not resolve; a Supervisor that relays every question is a dispatcher.
6. Questions that stop nothing wait for each other: one message carries all that gathered since
   the last, at a workstream boundary or when the room would otherwise stand still.
