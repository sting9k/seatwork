# Lenses

You ask; the lenses answer; you decide. A lens is a read-only Peer (spec `review`, `arch` or `search`) that sees only its brief.

## How many

- **One**: advice. Before you trust it, a Peer runs the check it proposes.
- **Two**: two different model families. Default.
- **Three**: add an angle. *Independent* reasons from first principles and recommends the strongest answer, exposing what it rests on. *Premise challenger* tests the framing and builds at least one viable alternative, without manufacturing disagreement. *Specialist* applies one named domain only, where that domain's rules decide.
- More only when the stakes are high. Lenses on one model family that agree are weak evidence.

## The brief

The same neutral text for every lens, plus one line with its angle:
- the question in the asker's words;
- facts with their sources, kept apart from unchecked claims and unknowns;
- hard constraints, kept apart from preferences;
- the sections to answer in: position, recommendation, the claims it rests on, best alternative, strongest counterargument, what would prove it wrong, confidence.

Nothing in it hints at the answer you prefer, and no lens sees another's answer.

## Read the answers

1. Reduce them to the three to five propositions that decide it; mark each verified, contested or unresolved.
2. Overlap (both agree, or both reject) is strong: accept it after checking any claim that is cheap to check.
3. A conflict that matters: send each lens the other's argument without saying which you prefer. It answers `CONCEDE`, `MAINTAIN`, `NARROW` or `REVERSE` with its evidence. One round; a second only if new evidence came in and the conflict still decides the outcome.
4. A disputed fact goes to a Peer to check one proposition; lenses never vote on facts.
5. Read the answers in reverse order once, so the first did not anchor you.
6. If the answers diverge wildly, the question was framed badly: reframe and ask again rather than averaging.

Sort each finding: **Act on** (real, blocks the work) · **Consider** (real, cost unclear) · **Noted** (valid, not now) · **Dismissed** (wrong or missing context, with why).

## Decide

Settle on evidence, never on which lens held out longer or on a count of votes. What stays unsettled stays open, and you say so. A lens neither edits nor decides. A decision that changes the outcome, the cost, an outside effect or an irreversible risk goes up as `DECISION_NEEDED`.

## Report

The decision and why; what the lenses agreed on; each conflict and how you settled it; what you rejected or left unproven; its limits, including a single model family; what would reopen it.
